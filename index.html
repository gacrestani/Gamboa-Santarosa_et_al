Interactive Manhattan plots

Genome-wide allele-frequency differentiation from Gamboa-Santarosa & Crestani et al., under review. Each point is a SNP; the x-axis is genomic position across chromosome arms 2L, 2R, 3L, 3R, X; the y-axis is −log10(p) from a Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel (CMH) test across replicate populations.

How to read: hover a highlighted SNP to see its per-replicate allele-frequency trajectory (O-type populations in blue, B-type in red). Graphs go from generation 1 → final. Asterisks mark SNPs also significant at the stricter FWER 0.01 threshold. Each panel's colour coding is given in its bottom legend.

  • O vs O (CMH)
    Allele-frequency change within the selected (O) regime, generation 1 → 20.
  • O vs B (CMH)
    Divergence between the selected (O) and control (B) regimes at the final generations.
  • B vs B (CMH)
    Allele-frequency change within the control (B) regime, generation 1 → 56.
  • O vs O (Adapted CMH)
    The O-regime test corrected for genetic drift (ACER, using effective population size).
